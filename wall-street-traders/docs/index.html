01 / Overview

What is Wall Street Traders?

Wall Street Traders is a collection of 444 fully revealed monochrome NFTs built with AI and individually curated by Commit Protocol. Every trader shares the same black suit while preserving a distinct identity through faces, silhouettes and special classes.

The collection is launching on Robinhood Chain through OpenSea. Its long-term direction is built around identity, creative evolution and transparent on-chain experimentation.

StonkBrokers turned NFTs into infrastructure. Normies turned NFTs into creative material. Wall Street Traders aims to turn NFTs into on-chain careers.

05 / Promotion Desk

Careers can become permanent.

The Promotion Desk is a proposed burn-to-evolve mechanic. A holder would make an irreversible decision to retire an original Wall Street Trader and mint an evolved NFT from a separate executive collection.

  • The original Trader would be permanently burned.
  • The promoted NFT would preserve the origin and ID of the retired Trader.
  • The first program is expected to be capped at a maximum of 44 promotions.
  • Final artwork would combine AI generation with individual human curation.
  • Exact rules, costs and contracts will be published before activation.

The Trader does not disappear. The Trader gets promoted.

This mechanism is not active at launch. Its final implementation remains subject to development, testing, security review and community feedback.

08 / FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

How many Wall Street Traders exist?

The fixed collection contains 444 NFTs.

When will the collection reveal?

The final artwork and metadata will reveal after the mint sells out — when the last trader clocks in.

Was the collection created with AI?

Yes. The artwork was built with AI and then manually curated, reviewed, corrected and organized by Commit Protocol.

Is the Promotion Desk active?

No. It is a planned roadmap concept. Full mechanics and contracts will be published before any holder is asked to burn an NFT.

Will Wall Street Traders launch a token?

A token is being explored, not promised. No token will be introduced before its utility, distribution, security and legal considerations are defined.

Do holders own the treasury?

No. Holding an NFT does not represent ownership of treasury assets, company equity, revenue or intellectual property beyond any rights explicitly published by the project.

09 / Disclaimer

Collect responsibly.

Wall Street Traders NFTs are digital collectibles. They do not represent equity, debt, ownership of treasury funds, guaranteed utility, revenue rights or a promise of financial return.

Roadmap items describe current intentions and may change as technical, security, legal or ecosystem conditions evolve. Participation in future mechanics, including burning, upgrading or token-related experiments, will always be optional and subject to separate terms when applicable.
