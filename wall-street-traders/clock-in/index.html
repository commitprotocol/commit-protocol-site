01 / CAREER ACTIVATION

HOW CLOCK IN
IS DESIGNED.

Clock In will verify ownership, identify the selected Trader and create a career record. The original NFT remains in the holder's wallet throughout the process.

01VERIFY OWNERSHIP

Confirm the holder.

Read the official WST contract to confirm which wallet currently owns the selected token ID.

02SELECT A TRADER

Choose the identity.

The holder selects which Wall Street Trader will begin an on-chain career.

03CREATE RECORD

Open the ledger.

A dedicated registry activates the Trader's career without modifying the original NFT contract.

04ENTER THE FLOOR

Prepare for seasons.

The career record becomes the foundation for future ranks, badges and Market Seasons.

02 / PROTOCOL ARCHITECTURE

ONE COLLECTION.
SEPARATE MODULES.

The deployed WST collection remains the permanent identity layer. New protocol features are designed as independent contracts around it, reducing unnecessary changes to the original NFTs.

DEPLOYEDWST NFT

Identity Contract

Confirms ownership, token IDs, supply and transfers. The original collection remains unchanged.

PHASE 03CAREER REGISTRY

Career Contract

Stores Lifetime Career Points, ranks, seasons, badges and milestones by WST token ID.

PHASE 04SEASON MANAGER

Competition Contract

Controls season participation and authorizes verified career updates under published rules.

RESEARCHWST TOKEN

Utility Contract

A separate ERC-20 research track. No token is deployed, allocated or promised at this stage.

RESEARCHREWARD VAULT

Claim Contract

Would manage eligible claims separately from NFT career history and prevent duplicate redemptions.

03 / TWO RECORDS

RARITY STAYS.
CAREERS EVOLVE.

Collection rarity and career progression serve different purposes. The permanent artwork traits never change, while a Trader's career can develop through participation.

PERMANENT RECORD

Collection Rarity

Calculated from the original artwork and metadata. It remains immutable throughout the life of the NFT.

  • Original traits
  • Rarity score and rank
  • Character and rarity tier
EVOLVING RECORD

Career Rank

Built from verified participation. A common Trader can develop a respected career without changing its original rarity.

  • Lifetime Career Points
  • Seasons, ranks and badges
  • Permanent career milestones

04 / SECURITY PRINCIPLES

THE NFT
STAYS PUT.

The planned architecture does not require the original NFT to be deposited, transferred or burned when a career is activated.

  • No custody of the holder's WST NFT
  • No approval to transfer the NFT for Clock In
  • No modification of the original collection contract
  • No guaranteed utility, yield, profit or financial return
  • Future contracts will be tested before public activation
