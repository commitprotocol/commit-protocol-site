04 / PORTFOLIO RULES

BUILD IT RIGHT.

The lock button only activates when every rule below is satisfied.
01

Every NFT can enter.

There is no wallet limit. Each eligible WST NFT creates one independent Trader entry and one portfolio.

02

Choose 3–5 assets.

Select at least three and no more than five of AAPL, AMZN, MSFT, NVDA and TSLA.

03

Use 10%–40% each.

Every selected asset needs a whole-number allocation between 10% and 40%.

04

Total exactly 100%.

The complete allocation must equal 100%, representing all US$ 10,000 of virtual capital.

05

Lock means final.

There is no rebalancing after submission. No real money, stocks or Stock Tokens are used.

06

Ranking is per NFT.

A wallet may hold several positions because every registered Trader competes separately.

03 / TWO POINT SYSTEMS

SEASONS PAY.
CAREERS REMEMBER.

Season Points determine one competition's funded reward share. Lifetime Career Points remain permanent, non-transferable progression and never create a fixed claim on WST.

SEASON RECORD

Season Points

Created from eligible performance during one Market Season. They determine the proportional share of that season's pre-funded pool.

  • Reset for each new season
  • Rules published before entry
  • Share = your points ÷ eligible points
PERMANENT RECORD

Career Points

Lifetime, non-transferable progression attached to the Trader identity. Career Points unlock status, not a guaranteed token conversion.

  • Ranks, badges and milestones
  • Season history and reputation
  • No fixed CP-to-WST exchange rate

04 / FAIRNESS PRINCIPLES

FUNDED FIRST.
RULES FIRST.

Clock In separates identity, competition and rewards. Every announced Season Pool must be deposited and verifiable before the season begins.

  • The holder's WST NFT remains in the holder's wallet
  • No real stocks or Stock Tokens are purchased or custodied
  • Primary leaderboards cannot be bought with WST
  • Reward shares follow published eligible Season Points
  • No guaranteed yield, profit, reward value or financial return
